# Project Halcyon — confidential pricing memo (FICTIONAL DEMO DOCUMENT)

Prepared for the Executive Pricing Committee. Do not forward outside the committee distribution list.

Halcyon Treasury will reprice its intraday liquidity facility on 1 February 2027. The committee recommends a tiered fee of 11 basis points on the first AUD 250 million of daily netted volume, falling to 6 basis points above AUD 1.2 billion, with a floor of AUD 18,500 per month for any client on the premium settlement window.

The repricing assumes that the three largest clients, which together account for 41 percent of facility revenue, accept a twelve-month lock-in in exchange for a 9 percent rebate. If two of the three decline, the model projects a revenue shortfall of AUD 6.4 million in the first year, which the committee proposes to recover through the new overnight sweep product launching in the second quarter.

Competitive intelligence indicates that two rival providers will hold their current pricing until at least mid-2027. The committee therefore recommends announcing the change to clients no earlier than 15 December 2026, to avoid giving competitors time to respond with targeted retention offers.
