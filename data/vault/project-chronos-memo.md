# Project Chronos — Q3 Board Memorandum (STRICTLY CONFIDENTIAL)

**To:** Board of Directors, Halverson Meridian Holdings
**From:** Office of Corporate Development
**Re:** Proposed acquisition of Corvane Logistics Group ("Target")
**Classification:** Board-restricted. Material non-public information. (Fictional dummy asset for Aionix Sentinel.)

## 1. Recommendation

Management recommends that the Board authorise a non-binding indicative offer for 100% of Corvane Logistics Group at an enterprise value of AUD 1.86 billion, representing 9.4x trailing EBITDA and a 31% premium to the thirty-day volume weighted average price. Funding would combine a AUD 900 million syndicated term loan, a AUD 450 million institutional placement and existing cash reserves. We propose that the offer be delivered to the Target chair no later than 14 October, ahead of the Target's scheduled investor day.

## 2. Strategic rationale

Corvane operates fourteen cold-chain distribution centres along the eastern seaboard and holds long-term service contracts with three of the four largest grocery retailers. Combining Corvane's network with our existing freight-forwarding platform eliminates duplicated linehaul capacity on the Brisbane to Melbourne corridor and gives us an immediate position in pharmaceutical cold-chain logistics, a segment our own growth plan did not expect to enter until FY28. Management estimates run-rate cost synergies of AUD 74 million by the end of the second full year, of which approximately 60 percent relates to network consolidation and the balance to procurement and shared corporate services.

## 3. Valuation and negotiation strategy

The indicative price has been set deliberately below our walk-away value of AUD 2.05 billion to preserve headroom for a competitive process. Our advisers believe a rival bid from a private equity consortium is likely if the approach becomes public. The negotiation team will therefore seek a six-week exclusivity period in exchange for a modest break fee of one percent of equity value. Under no circumstances should the walk-away value, the synergy estimate or the existence of Project Chronos be disclosed outside the deal team, the Board and our appointed advisers.

## 4. Key risks

The principal risks are customer concentration, because the two largest Corvane contracts account for 38 percent of revenue and both contain change-of-control clauses; integration risk across two incompatible warehouse management systems; and regulatory review by the competition regulator in the refrigerated freight segment, where the combined entity would hold an estimated 27 percent share. A preliminary legal view suggests behavioural undertakings may be required. Due diligence has also identified a contingent liability of up to AUD 22 million relating to an ongoing workplace safety investigation at the Target's Dandenong facility.

## 5. Workforce and communications

The integration plan assumes the consolidation of four overlapping distribution centres and a reduction of approximately 410 roles over eighteen months, partially offset by new roles in the pharmaceutical division. No employee communications will occur before signing. A holding statement has been prepared for use only in the event of a market leak, and trading in Target securities by any person aware of Project Chronos is prohibited under the company's securities trading policy.

## 6. Resolutions sought

The Board is asked to approve the indicative offer parameters described above, authorise the Chief Executive Officer and Chief Financial Officer to negotiate exclusivity and a confidentiality deed, and approve expenditure of up to AUD 6.5 million on external advisers for confirmatory due diligence. A final binding proposal will be presented to the Board for approval before any transaction documents are executed.
